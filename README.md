One funny, true line reviewing the most boring everyday object: the humble coaster.

> "It sits under a mug. That is the job. It does it perfectly every day, and nobody has ever once said thank you."

The hook is the object's own dullness. A coaster does exactly one tiny job — sit under a mug — and it does that job perfectly, every single day, forever, without a word of thanks. The turn comes early ("That is the job."), and the closing words sharpen it: nobody has ever once said thank you.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

This writes the line (110 characters) to `/output/entry.txt`.
